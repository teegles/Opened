# Fold Count privacy policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Fold Count does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app
does not contain advertising, analytics, trackers, or an internet permission.

Fold Count reads the device hinge-angle sensor and whether the screen is
interactive. It stores fold counts and opened/closed screen-time totals in the
app's private local storage. Other apps cannot read this storage through normal
Android APIs. Fold Count does not inspect which apps you use.

The app requests notification permission so Android can display the persistent
notification required for background tracking. It also requests permission to
restart tracking after a device reboot. These permissions are not used to
collect data.

Automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. Uninstalling
Fold Count removes its locally stored statistics.

Questions or concerns can be filed publicly through the
[Fold Count issue tracker](https://github.com/teegles/Fold-Count/issues).
