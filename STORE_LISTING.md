## Full description

Fold Count is a simple, private tracker for foldable phones. It counts how
often you unfold your phone and compares screen-on time while the device is
opened versus closed.

View today, the past seven days, or all-time totals. Everything stays on your
phone. Fold Count has no internet, location, or app-usage access.

Fold Count was designed for the OnePlus Open. Hinge detection and fold counting
have also been confirmed on a Samsung Galaxy Z Flip7. Other devices need
testing and must expose Android's standard hinge-angle sensor.
